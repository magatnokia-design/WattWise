Before you start

What you need

  • The Dagitab Hub
  • Your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi name and password
  • A phone with the Dagitab app installed
Mains inside · in an emergency

Never open the unit while it is plugged in. It carries mains voltage for the two switched outlets. Nothing inside is meant to be adjusted — the one exception is changing the Wi-Fi network, and page 7 says how to do that safely. If the housing or a lead is damaged, or either has been wet, leave it unplugged and have it checked.

Fire, smoke or a burning smell: switch off at the wall only if it is safe to reach, leave the room, then call.

How it works

A Hub with no Wi-Fi saved creates its own temporary network. Join it with your phone, tell the Hub which router to use, and it restarts onto your network. Then pair it to your account in the app.

Nothing is pre-programmed

Your Wi-Fi password is never compiled into the firmware. It is entered here, on the day, and stored on the unit.

1

Power on the Hub

Plug the Hub into a wall socket and wait about ten seconds. It powers itself from the mains — there is no separate adapter. The setup network appears on its own; no button to press.

2

Join the setup network

In your phone's Wi-Fi settings pick the network above and enter its password. A “Sign in to network” notice appears within seconds — tap it. If nothing appears, browse to http://192.168.4.1

3

Choose your network

Pick your router from the list and type your Wi-Fi password. Nothing listed? Tap Rescan networks. The Hub sees 2.4 GHz only. Hidden router? Type its name into the hidden network box.

4

Save and connect

Tap Save and connect. The Hub restarts and joins your network; the setup network closes. If the password was wrong, it reopens so you can try again.

Troubleshooting6

Moving house, or a new router

Change the Wi-Fi network

Unplug from the wall first — this is not optional

Never do this with the Hub plugged into a mains socket. Power it from a USB-A 5 V 3 A adapter plugged into the ESP32 board inside. That runs the electronics only and leaves both outlets dead while you work.

For a moment during every restart the control lines sit undriven, and the switching board reads that as on. On mains that briefly energises an outlet with your hands inside the unit. On USB there is no mains behind the contacts.

1

Unplug it from the wall

Unplug the two outlets as well, then the Hub's own plug. Wait five seconds.

2

Open the lid

Safe only now it is unplugged. Inside you will see the ESP32 — the small board with a USB socket on one edge. Leave every wire alone.

3

Plug the adapter into the ESP32

USB-A 5 V 3 A into the ESP32's own socket, not the wall. Wait ten seconds. A phone charger of that rating is fine; a laptop port is not.

Hold BOOT for five seconds

Count it out. A quick tap does nothing, on purpose. The Hub then clears its Wi-Fi and restarts. Nothing happened? You let go early — try again.

5

Set up the new network

The setup network reappears in about ten seconds. Follow Part 1 from step 2.

6

Close it and plug it back in

Once the app shows the Hub reporting, unplug the USB, close the lid, and return the Hub to its wall socket.

Your pairing is not affected

Only the Wi-Fi is cleared. No need to scan page 5 again, and your history, budgets and timers are safe — they live in your account, not on the unit.
